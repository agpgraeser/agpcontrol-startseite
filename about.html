Prof. Dr.-Ing. Axel Graeser

Deutsche Fassung

Control engineering from four perspectives:

  • eight years heading software development for automation systems in industry,
  • 26 years as a professor – the last 22 at the University of Bremen as head of Process Automation and Robotics,
  • since 1990 a lecturer at Haus der Technik in Essen.
  • Today an independent trainer and consultant for control loop design, among others for Siemens AG.
